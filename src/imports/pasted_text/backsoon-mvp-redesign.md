I need you to redesign the BackSoon MVP website from scratch.

IMPORTANT: Do NOT create a generic SaaS landing page.

The previous design was too generic: generic green text, generic cards, no memorable logo, no visual storytelling, and no meaningful interaction.

BackSoon needs to feel like a real startup with a recognizable identity and a memorable product experience.

The website should tell a STORY.

A visitor should understand the problem emotionally and visually before being introduced to the solution.

==================================================
THE CORE STORY
==============

Imagine a university student named Adam.

Adam works part-time at a café.

One day, he gets an opportunity to go away for two weeks for an internship / university opportunity.

He is excited.

But then he remembers:

"I have shifts scheduled."

Now he has a problem.

He needs someone trustworthy to temporarily cover his shifts.

He starts messaging friends.

Nobody is available.

He checks WhatsApp.

Nothing.

He asks colleagues.

Still nothing.

He searches Facebook groups.

Too much noise.

He starts worrying that he might have to cancel his opportunity or create a problem for his employer.

THEN:

BackSoon.

He posts the temporary shift.

BackSoon finds suitable verified students.

The employer chooses a replacement.

The shift is covered.

Adam can leave.

Two weeks later:

Adam comes back.

His responsibility is waiting for him.

This is the emotional story that the website should communicate.

==================================================
CORE MESSAGE
============

The central message is:

"Going away shouldn't mean leaving your responsibilities behind."

Alternative concept:

"You can go away. Your shift doesn't have to."

BackSoon connects businesses with verified students who can temporarily cover work shifts when regular workers are away or unavailable.

The MVP is specifically about temporary student work/shift replacement.

Do NOT introduce rooms, pets, childcare, or other categories into the primary website experience.
